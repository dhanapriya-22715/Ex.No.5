

# EXP 5: COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF DIFFERENT TYPES OF PROMPTING PATTERNS AND EXPLAIN WITH VARIOUS TEST SCENARIOS


## Aim

To test and compare how different prompting pattern models respond to various prompts such as broad or unstructured prompts versus refined prompts across multiple scenarios like Zero-shot prompting, Few-shot prompting, and Chain-of-Thought prompting. The experiment analyzes the quality, accuracy, and depth of the generated responses.


# AI Tool Used

* [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

# Explanation

Prompt engineering is the process of designing instructions that help AI models generate better responses. Different prompting techniques influence the quality and depth of outputs.

### 1. Basic Prompt

A simple and direct instruction given to the AI.

### 2. Zero-Shot Prompting

The AI performs the task without any examples.

### 3. Few-Shot Prompting

The AI is given a few examples before performing the task.

### 4. Chain-of-Thought Prompting

The AI explains the reasoning process step by step before arriving at the final answer.

# Activity 1: Educational Explanation

## Task

Explain Artificial Intelligence.


# Basic Prompt

“Explain Artificial Intelligence.”

### Output

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and decision-making.



# Zero-Shot Prompt

“Explain Artificial Intelligence in simple language for school students.”

### Output

Artificial Intelligence is when computers or machines are designed to think and learn like humans. Examples include voice assistants, robots, and self-driving cars.


# Few-Shot Prompt

### Prompt

Example 1:
Question: Explain the Internet.
Answer: The Internet is a global network that connects computers and allows people to share information.

Example 2:
Question: Explain Cloud Computing.
Answer: Cloud computing allows users to store and access data through the internet instead of a personal computer.

Now explain Artificial Intelligence in a similar way.

### Output

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that allows machines to think, learn, and solve problems like humans. AI is used in chatbots, virtual assistants, healthcare systems, and smart devices.



# Chain-of-Thought Prompt

### Prompt

“Explain Artificial Intelligence step by step by first defining intelligence, then explaining how machines imitate human thinking, and finally provide examples.”

### Output

1. Intelligence refers to the ability to learn, understand, and solve problems.
2. Artificial Intelligence means creating machines that can imitate human intelligence.
3. These machines use data and algorithms to make decisions.
4. AI is used in voice assistants, online recommendations, and medical diagnosis systems.
5. Therefore, AI helps machines perform tasks that usually require human thinking.

# Activity 2: Creative Story Generation

## Task

Generate a short story.


## Basic Prompt

“Write a short story.”

### Output

A boy found a mysterious key in the forest and discovered a hidden treasure chest.


## Zero-Shot Prompt

“Write a short adventure story about a lost robot.”

### Output

A robot lost in a desert searched for its creator while helping travelers along the way.


## Few-Shot Prompt

### Prompt

Example 1:
Story: A girl found a magical book that could predict the future.

Example 2:
Story: A scientist created a machine that could travel through time.

Now write a short story about a lost robot.

### Output

A robot named XR-5 got separated from its spaceship. While searching for home, it saved villagers from a storm and became their hero.


## Chain-of-Thought Prompt

### Prompt

“Write a story step by step by introducing the character, describing the problem, explaining the journey, and ending with a solution.”

### Output

1. A robot named Nova was left behind after a spaceship accident.
2. Nova searched for its crew across dangerous mountains.
3. Along the journey, Nova helped people repair machines and rescue animals.
4. Finally, the spaceship returned and found Nova.
5. Nova reunited with the crew and was rewarded for bravery.

# Analysis

## Basic Prompt

* Produces short and direct responses.
* Suitable for simple tasks.
* Limited detail and creativity.

## Zero-Shot Prompt

* Gives better results because instructions are clearer.
* No examples are required.
* Produces relevant outputs quickly.

## Few-Shot Prompt

* Examples guide the AI toward the expected format.
* Improves consistency, creativity, and response quality.

## Chain-of-Thought Prompt

* Encourages logical reasoning and step-by-step explanations.
* Produces the deepest and most detailed responses.
* Useful for problem-solving and educational tasks.



# Findings

1. Prompt refinement improves the quality of AI-generated responses.
2. Zero-shot prompting works well for straightforward tasks.
3. Few-shot prompting improves structure and consistency through examples.
4. Chain-of-thought prompting provides the most detailed and logical outputs.
5. The effectiveness of AI responses depends heavily on prompt clarity and structure.



# Conclusion

The experiment successfully compared different prompting techniques including Basic, Zero-shot, Few-shot, and Chain-of-Thought prompting. The results showed that structured prompting methods significantly improve response quality, depth, and clarity. Among all techniques, Chain-of-Thought prompting produced the most detailed and logical responses, while Few-shot prompting improved consistency through examples.



# RESULT

The prompting techniques were tested successfully, and the analysis showed that refined prompting patterns generate more accurate, clear, and detailed responses compared to simple or unstructured prompts.
